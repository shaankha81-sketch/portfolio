About me

I work as an Agentic and Robotic AI Trainer and C++ is the language I code in: I check how AI systems answer, reason and plan, and I mark exactly where they go wrong.

C++ is my main programming language. I practise problem solving and data structures in it on HackerRank and LeetCode, and I use Python and JavaScript for projects such as RajAtlas, an interactive atlas of Rajasthan, and this website.

I am in the final year of a B.A. at Maharaja Surajmal Brij University, Bharatpur (Geography, Sociology, Political Science). That background gives me a careful eye for maps, numbers and sources. I read and write English, Hindi and Urdu, so I can review content in all three.
