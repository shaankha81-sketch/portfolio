01Profile

About me

I work as an Agentic and Robotic AI Trainer: I check how AI systems answer, reason and plan, and I mark exactly where they go wrong.

I am in the final year of a B.A. at Maharaja Surajmal Brij University, Bharatpur. Geography is my main subject — climate, oceans, regions, map projections, surveying and statistics. Sociology and Political Science add an understanding of society, institutions and research methods.

Alongside college I practise Class 10–12 mathematics on my own, checking every step and every answer. I read and write English, Hindi and Urdu, so I can review content in all three.

What my AI training work involves

  • Evaluating AI answers on geography and the social sciences
  • Map-based and spatial reasoning tasks
  • Visual data annotation
  • Reviewing robot action sequences step by step
  • Writing clear notes that justify every rating

Strengths I bring to AI training

Spatial reasoning

Reviewing location-based explanations, reading maps and judging descriptions of physical and human environments.

Observation & description

Spotting visible details and organising them into clear descriptions for visual annotation and action-sequence review.

Analytical review

Checking whether an explanation is correct, logically ordered and consistent from the first step to the last.

Research & communication

Academic reading, comparing concepts and writing organised explanations that document each review decision.

Three languages

Reviewing meaning, grammar and tone in English, Hindi and Urdu, including right-to-left Urdu text.

Independent learning

Self-directed mathematics practice, multidisciplinary study and a real interest in learning project guidelines and evaluation criteria.
