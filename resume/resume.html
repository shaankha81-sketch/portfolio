Experience

Agentic & Robotic AI Trainer Current

  • Response evaluation. Review AI answers on geography and the social sciences for factual accuracy, correct reasoning and clarity, and rate them against project rubrics.
  • Robotic action sequences. Review robot task plans step by step: is every step in a workable order, does each step have what it needs before it runs, and does the physics (force, friction, distance, torque) allow it?
  • Error analysis. Find the first incorrect step in a multi-step answer and classify the error as fact, calculation, units or method, since later mistakes often follow from one early slip.
  • Spatial and map-based tasks. Check directions, distances, scale, layout and whether a described route, scene or object arrangement is physically possible.
  • Visual annotation. Describe what is visible in an image or scene in a structured, consistent way for annotation.
  • Written justification. Write short, precise review notes that explain each rating and follow the project guidelines exactly.

How I review an answer

  • Read the guidelines first and apply them exactly as written, not as I would prefer them.
  • Re-do the problem independently before looking at the model's solution.
  • Check each step on its own; mark the first wrong step and note which later steps are wrong only because of it.
  • Watch for answers that look right but use a wrong method, or sound confident while giving a wrong fact.
  • Score accuracy, reasoning and clarity separately, then explain the score in one or two sentences.
